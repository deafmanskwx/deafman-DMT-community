# DMT 2.9.1 Release Notes

In Tracks, manual **ALL** now applies colours in the order you visit the swatches. You can also browse **CFG** by scrolling and add rules in **MAP** without splitting up a converted group.

**After updating, replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your set.** Drag the bundled device from the **amxd** handle in Tracks to use the updated colour ranges.

## Manual colour ranges

- With **ALL** in manual mode, hold **Shift** and scroll through the swatches you want. The range follows your path, including across the edge of the grid. Scroll back to an earlier swatch to shorten the range, then release **Shift** to apply it.
- When you choose more swatches than you have tracks to colour, the range includes both ends. For example, five swatches across three tracks use the first, middle and last colours. A single track takes the first colour.

## MAP rules and CFG navigation

- In **MAP**, **ADD RULE** opens the new rule and scrolls it into view. Rules made from selected tracks stay in the order you added them, outside the converted group frames. Tracks from the same Live group can share a standalone rule.
- In **CFG**, use the mouse wheel or a vertical trackpad gesture to move one section at a time. When the pointer is over **SHORTCUTS**, scroll to read the list. At the top or bottom, scroll again in the same direction to move to the adjacent section.
