DMT 2.8.0

Much faster Tracks colour updates, with automatic clip colouring.

  • Tracks performance: track colours refresh almost in real time while you drag the sensitivity slider, with substantially faster updates across your Live set.
  • Automatic clip colouring: Command-click CLP to turn it on or off. The CLP label lights up while enabled.
  • Session and Arrangement: clips follow colours applied to their tracks by DMT, including palette and sensitivity adjustments.
  • Track commands: automatic clip colouring also follows SEL, ALL, audio/MIDI commands, group colouring and MAP rules.
  • Smoother adjustments: rapid edits apply the latest clip colour and skip unnecessary clip work.
  • Saved preferences: automatic colouring uses your existing clip dimming and is remembered separately for each harmony directive.
  • Manual control: CLP choices take precedence until the next track-colour or dimming change. 2TRK preserves existing clip-colour variations when taking the track colour from its clips.

Live device update: replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your Live set with the device included in this version.
