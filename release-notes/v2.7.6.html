DMT 2.7.6

More reliable saved theme setups, content updates, and Tracks colour matching.

  • Saved setups: retain groups, preset selections, and macro assignments through export, import, and reopening, with separate saved state for Standard and Z-Depth.
  • Batch copies and group renaming: retain saved edits and preset selections.
  • Content updates: retain your setup edits, preserve the library when an update fails, and report incomplete imports accurately.
  • Tracks settings: keep separate saved setups for each colour directive.
  • Tracks colour matching: account for theme brightness and keep the closest available colour at maximum sensitivity, respecting disabled swatches and colour-family filters.
  • Tracks background refresh: keep your global setting across themes, colour directives, and presets.
  • Tracks analysis controls: respond more smoothly to dragging across source labels.
  • Colour operator insertion: clear active searches and bring the added operator into view from menus and Library drops.
