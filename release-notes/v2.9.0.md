# DMT 2.9.0 Release Notes

Tracks can now share one colouring setup across themes or recall a preset supplied with a theme. MAP follows the group structure of your Ableton Live set.

**After updating, replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your set.** Drag the bundled device from the **amxd** handle in Tracks. If you have more than one Live 12 installation open, Tracks sends commands to the installation selected in DMT.

## Tracks setups and palettes

- In **CFG > SETUP**, choose **GLOBAL** to share settings across themes or **THIS THEME** to keep a personal override. Switching back to Global keeps that override for later. Standalone `.ask` files each have their own setup. When macOS supplies a document ID, the setup follows the file if you rename it.
- To recall a supplied Tracks setup, turn on **Enable Tracks Presets** in **Settings > Content**, then choose **CONTENT PRESET** for that theme.
- With **AUTO** and **ALL** active, **LAYOUT** assigns enabled swatches to successive tracks in grid order. Choose **FACTORY** for hue families or **GRADED** for lightest to darkest. Option-scroll to shift the starting colour. With **GRP** active, the offset shifts member colours while keeping the group headers in place.
- Choose which theme colours shape each harmony directive in **CFG > ANALYSIS (ADV)**. In Tuneables, drag the weight bars to give sources such as the selection colour or the VU meters more or less influence.
- Hold **Space** to reveal every hue in the enabled brightness columns when picking colours for **ALL**, **SEL**, **AUD**, **MID** or **GRP**. To apply a range that includes those revealed colours, release **Shift** before **Space**.

## Groups, menus and shortcuts

- In **MAP**, **CONVERT** shows groups and nested groups in Live track order. Each header has its own rule, even when two headers share a colour. **UPDATE** refreshes captured colours while keeping your keyword rules, manual overrides and multi-track selections.
- The **Options** menu puts harmony directives, track and group colour schemes, and clip sort order beside the palette. Assign up to four shortcuts on the second Tuneables page. For horizontal clip ranges, choose **LINEAR**, **REVERSE** or **CYCLE**. Selecting an active **CYCLE** again steps its multiplier from 1× through 9×.
- Choose **Mouse Wheel** or **Gesture** under **CFG > SETUP > Menu Nav** for Options and the **CLP** menu. In Gesture mode, move the mouse forward or back to highlight a row, then click to confirm.

## Documentation

In the Library’s documentation view, search for a topic, an operator name or one of its controls. All 14 factory Colour Operators have individual guides. The updated user manual and in-app help group the Tracks shortcuts by where you use them. The companion UI Atlas gives each of the manual’s 28 figures its own bookmarked page.
