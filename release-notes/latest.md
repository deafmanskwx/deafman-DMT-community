# DMT 2.9.6 Release Notes

In Tracks, open **MAP** and use the **#** colour picker to sample a group header or member track in Live. The matching group opens in MAP. Sample the same colour again to cycle through matches.

**If you use Tracks, replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your set after updating.** Drag the new device from the **amxd** handle in Tracks.

## Tracks

- Click the arrow beside **CONVERT** or **UPDATE** and turn on **Include loose tracks** to capture the colours of ungrouped audio and MIDI tracks too. Saved MAP presets remember this setting.
- When groups would share a colour, automatic colouring looks for an unused swatch that matches the theme as well or better and fits your palette sort order.
- Tracks detects duplicate DMT-Tracks devices more reliably, including when an older copy is loaded alongside the current one. Keep one current device in your set to resume colouring.

## Editor

- Reverse colour search swatches update as you edit the theme. For transparent colours, the swatch also reflects edits to the background used for the preview.
