# DMT 2.9.5 Release Notes

To use your Live 12 themes in Ableton Live 13 Beta, select **Live 13 Beta** at the top of the Library and load a theme as usual. DMT converts it when you load it and keeps the Live 12 original. If a theme pack includes a Live 13 version, DMT loads that version directly.

**DMT’s support for Live 13 is also in beta.** Some colours or contrasts may look slightly off in converted themes, especially on controls new to Live 13.

**If you use Tracks, replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your set after updating.** Drag the new device from the **amxd** handle in Tracks.

## Tracks

- Click the padlock on a **MAP** rule to keep it active when MAP is off. Locking a group also protects its members, so you can recolour the rest of the set without overwriting the group’s MAP colours.
- Using **GRP** to apply a colour manually changes just the group headers, even when automatic group colouring is armed.

## Editor and Engine

- Reverse colour search shows the sampled colour alongside the matches. For a transparent match, the swatch shows the estimated blend and its tooltip names the background used. The floating **Parameter Picker** shows the same ranked matches.
- Close **Edit History** to return to the same place in the Editor.
- **Swatch Memory** and **Colour History** snap to whole rows as you scroll and remember where you were when you switch between them or resize the swatches.
- When you create or rename a macro, the name field is ready to type in. Press **Return** to confirm or **Escape** to cancel.

## Settings

- **Version Info** shows the installed DMT version and build number. Click **Show Splash** to see the startup screen without hiding your workspace.
