DMT 2.8.7

A hotfix for window zoom in Tracks.

  • Palette and sidebar clicks now land on the intended control at every zoom level. The Tracks panel fits between the header and footer after a zoom change.
  • Hold Shift to reveal the full palette while choosing colours with SEL or ALL.
  • In MAP, use ADD RULE to group selected Live tracks into one rule. Colours applied with SEL are saved for those tracks.

To use the updated MAP controls, replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your Live set with the copy included in this release.
