DMT 2.7.4

New colour operators and immediate Grade refresh in Ableton Live.

  • Invert: blend colours towards their RGB negative from 0% to 100%, preserving transparency. Available below Blend in the operator menu.
  • Vibrance: boost muted colours more than saturated accents, or reduce colourfulness, from -100% to +100%. Neutral greys stay neutral.
  • Both operators display percentages and start at 0%, leaving the current appearance unchanged.
  • With Autorefresh enabled, completed Grade edits refresh Live immediately in Ultra, Aggressive, and Conservative modes, without a second delayed refresh.
