DMT 2.9.0

Tracks adds shared colouring setups, per-theme presets and a MAP view that follows your Live set’s group structure.

  • Choose GLOBAL, THIS THEME or an enabled CONTENT PRESET in Tracks > CFG > SETUP. Standalone .ask themes can each keep their own colour settings.
  • MAP shows converted groups and nested groups in Live track order. Each group header can have its own colour rule.
  • With AUTO and ALL active, LAYOUT applies the palette in grid order. Use GRADED to colour successive tracks from lightest to darkest.
  • Assign up to four Options shortcuts for directives, track and group colours, or clip sort order. Navigate Options and the clip menu with the mouse wheel or a forward/back gesture.
  • Search the in-app documentation by topic, operator or control. It includes guides to all 14 factory Colour Operators and a Tracks shortcut reference.

After updating, replace DMT-Tracks in your Live set. Drag the new Max for Live device from the amxd handle in Tracks.
