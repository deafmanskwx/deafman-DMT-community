DMT 2.8.5

  • Colouring defaults: choose TONAL or SPECTRAL in Tracks > CFG > SETUP for automatic track and group colouring in Tracks and Tweek. The default is saved across restarts; existing themes retain their saved schemes when recalled.
  • Detailed AutoSetup: added Grade Exposure, Contrast and Saturation controls, plus separate Override Red, Green and Blue controls.
  • Macro mappings: custom ranges, curves, inversion and Flip settings are retained through editing and reloading. Override colour channels can also be used in macros and custom colour operators.
  • Macro bypass: removing a bypassed macro or its last route restores the previous operator state. Shared bypasses remain active until the last bypass is released.
  • Library tabs: more responsive switching between Library, Tracks and Matrix, with scroll positions retained.
