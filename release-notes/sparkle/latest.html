DMT 2.8.3

Shift hue selectively by saturation or lightness, and keep manually chosen clip colours when tracks are recoloured.

  • Selective Property: a new colour operator selects colours above a saturation or luma threshold, with adjustable hue shift and soft spread.
  • Macro control: assign Shift, Threshold and Spread to macros, or include Selective Property in custom colour operators.
  • KEEP CURRENT: with automatic clip colouring and PRESERVE EDITS enabled in Tracks > CFG > CLIP COLOURING, the main clip group follows the track while other groups retain their current colours, including later manual edits.
  • Preservation choices: KEEP CURRENT is the initial choice. Existing saved choices are retained, and toggling PRESERVE EDITS remembers the last selection.

Live device update: replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your Live set with the device included in this version to use KEEP CURRENT.
