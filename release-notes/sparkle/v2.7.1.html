DMT 2.7.1

This update improves Auto Setup, theme saving and recovery, and editor navigation. It also reduces repeated calculations in the Perception Map and Tracks.

  • Cmd–H opens edit History; Escape closes it. Double-click a parameter or group search result to confirm it and reveal the selection.
  • The Operators browser remembers the choice between Factory and Custom operators between sessions.
  • Reset Slider now returns Auto Setup sliders to their neutral 50% position. Z-Depth group resets also centre the selected sliders when an assignment cannot update its target.
  • Saved Auto Setup slider positions and colour edits restore correctly when reopening a theme. Simple controls stay in Simple mode, and switching themes or setup modes keeps each theme's colours and controls separate.
  • Theme versions restore their own controls and colours, including older flat-layout snapshots. Switching versions saves departing edits first and stops if that save fails. Deleted snapshots stay removed after imports.
  • Snapshot creation reports save failures and keeps current edits available for retry. Undo after a manual colour edit retains the preceding macro or operator changes.
  • Recover Current Edits saves the current session when its working copy is unreadable, backing up that file and preserving the previous readable checkpoint. Removing active content also saves pending edits first.
  • Engine colour edits refresh Live immediately with Autorefresh enabled. Aggressive refresh sends a restored theme once, and queued updates keep their order.
  • The Perception Map and Tracks reuse unchanged calculations. The map stops updating while the Engine pane is hidden after its initial layout, and calibration-marker edits finish when a Shift-drag ends or the panel closes.
  • The § reverse-search shortcut recognizes the focused parameter search field. Left and right arrows retain normal text-cursor movement.
