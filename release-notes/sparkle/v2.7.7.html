DMT 2.7.7

Improved Tracks colour ordering and responsiveness, clip dimming, and saved AutoSetup controls.

  • Tracks colour ordering: apply HUE, SPREAD, or PING-PONG order and repetition frequency to tracks and group members, including nested groups.
  • Tracks responsiveness: faster command pickup in Live, fewer repeated track and clip scans, and reuse of unchanged theme analysis.
  • Group colour directives: update group headers and members when switching directives with automatic group colouring enabled.
  • Clip dimming: apply changes even when the track already has the requested colour.
  • Saved AutoSetup controls: restore macro selections such as Temperature to match the saved colours when reopening themes or restoring snapshots.
