DMT 2.9.3

If you use Tracks, replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your set after updating. Drag the new device from the amxd handle in Tracks.

  • In MAP, CONVERT saves Live’s group and member colours. AUTO restores the capture, OVER recalls your manual swatch, and CFG follows your current colouring settings.
  • COL and EXP keep the selected MAP rule. A group header highlights its group. A member highlights its own row.
  • With AUTO and ALL active in Tracks, PING-PONG and SPREAD follow the selected FACTORY or GRADED palette.
  • In Tracks Options, turn track or group colouring on and off, or choose LINK TO TRACK so groups follow the track settings.
  • Start Quick Start from Settings with Tracks open and keep your window layout. When a lesson needs a theme, you can load one from the Library or continue without one.
  • The source and destination colour swatches show transparency over a checkerboard.
  • Search help for custom operator or Custom OP. The manual and UI Atlas use the same terminology.
