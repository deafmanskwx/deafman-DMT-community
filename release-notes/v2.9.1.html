DMT 2.9.1

Tracks gives you finer control over manual colour ranges and a quicker way to browse CFG.

  • In manual ALL, Shift-scroll to pick swatches in the order you want them applied. Scroll back to shorten the range. A range of five swatches across three tracks uses the first, middle and last colours.
  • In MAP, ADD RULE opens the new rule in view. Rules for selected tracks stay outside converted groups, in the order you added them.
  • Scroll through CFG one section at a time. Inside SHORTCUTS, scroll through the list, then scroll again at either end to move to the next or previous section.

After updating, replace DMT-Tracks in your Live set. Drag the new Max for Live device from the amxd handle in Tracks.
