DMT 2.7.5

Improvements to colour swapping, reverse search, and AutoSetup navigation.

  • Colour swapping: exchange displayed colours and transparency with operators active, preserving operators and group locks. Swaps undo as one change; unsuccessful swaps leave both colours unchanged.
  • Reverse search: use the shortcut from either Colours or Blend search. Starting from Blend returns to Colours and opens the colour picker.
  • AutoSetup: the macro panel follows each theme's saved step, including empty steps.
  • The Engine status bar remains fully visible after returning from AutoSetup.
