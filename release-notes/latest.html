DMT 2.9.5

Select Live 13 Beta in the Library to load your themes in Ableton Live 13 Beta. DMT uses the Live 13 version when available or converts the Live 12 theme and keeps the original.

DMT’s support for Live 13 is also in beta. Some colours or contrasts may look slightly off in converted themes, especially on controls new to Live 13.

If you use Tracks, replace the DMT-Tracks Max for Live device in your set after updating. Drag the new device from the amxd handle in Tracks.

  • Lock a MAP rule to keep it active when MAP is off. Locking a group protects its members too.
  • Manual GRP colouring changes just the group headers.
  • Reverse colour search shows the sampled colour and estimated blends for transparent matches. Edit History keeps your place in the Editor.
  • Version Info in Settings shows your installed version and build number.
