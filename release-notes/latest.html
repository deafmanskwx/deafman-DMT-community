DMT 2.7.3

This hotfix removes the delay before colour-operator and blend edits reach Ableton Live when Autorefresh is enabled.

  • Colour-operator sliders refresh Live as soon as they are released. Blend-value edits refresh immediately in Ultra, Aggressive, and Conservative modes.
  • Rapid consecutive edits keep the latest state without a second, delayed refresh from the save timer.
  • Save failures retain the current edits, report the error, and keep the scheduled retry.
